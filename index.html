Questions

A few things you might want to know.

Still not sure if I can help? Get in touch and describe what you have in mind.

Can I book on behalf of a relative?

Yes. You can arrange and pay for the visit, and I'll send you a summary of the work afterwards.

Can you build a gaming PC?

Yes. Share your budget and what you'll use it for, and I'll recommend a parts list. You buy the parts in your name so the warranties are yours, and I assemble, set up and test the system.

What happens if a PC part is faulty?

Every build is tested before handover. If a component is faulty, I'll identify it and help you return it under the manufacturer's warranty.

Can you recover lost files?

Often, yes, if the drive still works. Data on a failed drive needs a specialist recovery service, and I'll tell you if that's the case.

Do you support businesses?

Yes. I can discuss small-business website projects and IT/networking work, including Wi-Fi, routers, switches and access points. Business work is scoped and quoted individually before anything starts.

Do you offer website hosting?

Hosting and ongoing website care can be discussed as part of a website project. The provider, responsibilities, renewal costs and support limits will be agreed in writing before you commit.

Do you have fixed prices for business projects?

Business websites and networking setups vary in scope, so I provide a quote after understanding your requirements. I'll explain the proposed work and costs before starting.
