Questions

Frequently asked questions

Is there a call‑out fee?

No. There's no call‑out fee, and if I can't fix the problem there's nothing to pay for my time.

Which areas do you cover?

Homes across the Dudley borough: Dudley, Stourbridge, Halesowen, Brierley Hill, Kingswinford and Sedgley.

Can I book on behalf of a relative?

Yes. You can arrange and pay for the visit, and I'll send you a summary of the work afterwards.

Can you build a gaming PC?

Yes. Share your budget and what you'll use it for, and I'll recommend a parts list. You buy the parts in your name so the warranties are yours, and I assemble, set up and test the system.

What happens if a PC part is faulty?

Every build is tested before handover. If a component is faulty, I'll identify it and help you return it under the manufacturer's warranty.

Can you recover lost files?

Often, yes, if the drive still works. Data on a failed drive needs a specialist recovery service, and I'll tell you if that's the case.

Do you support businesses?

I work with homes only. Businesses are better served by a dedicated IT support company.
